T4 — house and furniture as Blender models (director: Claude; read development/topdown-plan.md fixed rules first).

Owner feedback on the T2 top-down build: the house looks empty, the furniture is small flat picture-boxes, and the floor wood pattern is too big and too red. Replace the Codex picture-box furniture with real low-poly Blender models and make the rooms feel lived-in. Story, dialogue, interactions, branches and endings stay exactly the same.

1. Blender (art-src/blender/make_props.py → godot/assets/models/prop_<name>.glb), same style as the T1 characters: low-poly, flat-shaded, vertex colours (COLOR_0), muted 1930s Silesian palette, origin at the floor centre of the footprint, front facing −Z, 300–1500 tris each. One model for every prop now used in godot/scripts/chapter1.gd (g.prop calls): workbench (with vice), stool, bicycle, drum, wheels, cabinet, parts box, range (kitchen stove), table, chair, dresser, basin, crate (plus two lid states: potato crate dug shallow / dug deep — separate GLBs prop_crate_shallow, prop_crate_deep or a separate lid object, whatever keeps set_top() working), shelf, barrel, sacks, woodpile, pump.
   Add extra dressing so the rooms do not look empty (no gameplay, just solid where large): e.g. bed or bench, wall shelf with jars and pots, coat hooks with coats, rug, tool rack on the workshop wall, crates and coal bucket in the cellar, firewood basket, lamp, curtains, potted plant, small cupboard. Keep walkways and every interaction spot reachable; re-run the bots to prove it.
   Scale: match the game, where actors use MODEL_SCALE 0.47 (Paul 1.75 m model ≈ 0.82 game units). Real-world furniture sizes, scaled the same way, so a table reaches Paul's hip and a chair seat his knee. If the current g.prop sizes are too small for real furniture, enlarge them and adjust the matching solid/solid_box collision.
   Preview renders to art-src/blender/renders/props_*.png (all props lined up next to Paul, and one top-down 3/4 view at the game camera angle).
2. Godot: load the GLBs instead of the Prop picture-box (keep prop.gd's API — x, y, z, hidden, set_top, sync, free_node, light — so chapter1.gd and the search/hide logic work unchanged; a lit shader like CHAR_SHADER for the vertex colours). Keep the Codex picture-box only as a fallback if a GLB is missing.
3. Floor: make the wood boards read at real scale (boards ~0.15 m wide relative to Paul) and less saturated / less red; keep the creaky-board marks visible. Use existing textures (repeat/UV scale and shader tint are fine; no new code-drawn graphics).
4. Verify: all four bot plans reach the same END lines; web export to dist-godot. Append a "T4" section to development/reports/T4_props.md with what was made and anything left.
