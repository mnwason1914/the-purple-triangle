7. tex_wall_cellar — damp dark grey stone-brick cellar wall with moss and water stains near the bottom.
8. tex_wall_garden — garden wall: red brick courses with darker mortar, a row of rough grey granite blocks along the bottom, flat stone coping on top.
9. tex_wall_gate — the same garden wall with a closed wooden plank garden gate in the middle (gently arched top, black iron hinges and latch).
10. tex_wall_facade — exterior house wall: rough ochre-cream plaster, no baseboard, slight weathering.
